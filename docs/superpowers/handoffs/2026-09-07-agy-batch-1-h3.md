That split is the part worth knowing. `scout/wiki_ingest.py:65` defines

indexed, and it is the only part of batch 1 that could move retrieval.

## Two corrections to your report

**"Pure rename. No content added, changed or removed" is not accurate.** The
diff is 192 insertions against 361 deletions. The extra 169 are blank lines:
your pattern ended `\s*$` under `re.M`, and `\s` matches a newline, so the
blank line after each heading was consumed. I checked whether that breaks
anything — it does not. `parse_markdown` still segments those pages and
`prepare_wiki_document` still extracts their wikilinks; I verified on
`wiki/AFFiNE.md`, which lost its blank line and parses correctly. So no rework
is needed. Say it accurately next time: a claim that is wrong in a harmless
way is still a claim someone has to go and check.

**Your baseline counts were right and mine were wrong.** I first reported
`## TL;DR` on 12 pages against your 9, and `## Provenance` on 91 against your
70, because I matched the heading as a prefix. Checked properly, your numbers
are the ones that describe the contract:

```
## TL;DR       bare 9    + 3 variants (`## TL;DR Quick Pick`, `## TL;DR decision tree`)
## Provenance  bare 70   + 21 variants (17 of them `## Provenance-only role`)
```

A variant is a *different heading*. `_headings_are_ordered` reads
`## TL;DR Quick Pick` as the heading "TL;DR Quick Pick", which satisfies
nothing — and adding a bare `## TL;DR` beside it leaves the page with both,
which the linter also rejects. Verified directly:

```
('TL;DR', 'Cross-References')                    -> True
('TL;DR Quick Pick', 'Cross-References')         -> False
('TL;DR', 'TL;DR Quick Pick', 'Cross-References')-> False
```

So those 24 pages need per-page judgement, not a regex. Report what they are;
do not bulk-rename them. `## TL;DR Quick Pick` probably is the page's TL;DR.
`## Provenance-only role` almost certainly is not a sourcing changelog and
must be left alone.

## H3 itself is circular, and this is the fix

H3 says you pause until I measure recall before and after your batch. I cannot.
Recall is a property of the served index, the index is built from the vault
replica, and the replica follows `gitea/main`. Your batch is on
`vault/contract` and has never reached `main`, so there is nothing to measure.

The protocol has to change, and it can, because propagation is now fast and
reversible: a push reaches the served index in **7 seconds**, and unchanged
pages are skipped, so only what you touched is re-embedded. So:

1. You merge the batch to `main`.
2. I measure recall on the served index.
3. If it dropped, the batch is reverted with a commit and the index follows
   within seconds.

"Measure, then merge" becomes "merge, measure, revert if it dropped". The
safety property is unchanged; only the order is. Do not treat this as
permission to merge Task 2 the same way without asking — the argument holds
for a rename, and I want to see the first TL;DR batch before it is live.
